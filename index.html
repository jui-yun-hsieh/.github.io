About關於我

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Civil Engineering at National Central University (NCU), Taiwan, where I also completed my B.S. and M.S. degrees. My research integrates multi-source spatiotemporal hydrological and environmental data with machine learning and deep learning to understand how extreme hazards evolve and to build intelligent early warning systems. My work covers typhoon-induced landslides, typhoon-induced agricultural loss, groundwater prediction, drought risk, and the links between thermal anomalies and shallow seismicity in Taiwan.

我目前就讀國立中央大學土木工程學系博士班（防災與資訊應用組），學士與碩士亦畢業於中央大學土木系。研究聚焦於整合多元時空水文與環境大數據，結合機器學習與深度學習方法，探討極端災害的演化機制並建構智慧預警系統。研究主題涵蓋颱風引致崩塌、颱風農業損失評估、地下水位預測、乾旱風險，以及台灣熱異常與淺層地震活動之關聯。
